Story Sleuth: Find the Element!

Welcome to Story Sleuth! Read each short description and identify the story element being described.

Learning Objectives

  • Identify story elements from simple descriptions.
  • Choose the correct element based on its characteristics.
  • Explain how story elements appear in a short narrative.

How to Play

  1. Read each clue on the screen.
  2. Choose the story element that matches the clue.
  3. Click the "Check Answer" button to see whether your answer is correct.
  4. Continue until you finish all five questions and view your score.
